Hello there! I am a Full-Stack Software Engineer and Researcher, currently working in the MedTech domain on robot-assisted treatment of cancers at Quantum Surgical in Montpellier, France (2025 – ).

My professional background is in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Software Engineering:

  • Postdoc researcher in HCI for territorial concertations in the SENS and SELMET teams at CIRAD and INRAE in Montpellier (2022 – 2024)
  • Researcher and teaching assistant in algorithmics, object-oriented programming and Web development at Centrale Lyon (2019 – 2022)
  • PhD titled "Improving software languages and libraries for programming interaction", supervised by Stéphane Huot at Inria Lille (2015 – 2019)
  • R&D engineer in Paris on developping the software for an embedded digital TV recorder (2012 – 2014)
  • MSc in engineering and HCI from KTH in Sweden and Centrale Marseille (2008 – 2012)

I do enjoy crafting impactful innovations in software, making educational talks to share them, and bringing them all the way to end users. In 2024 I reduced my active involvements in public academic research to focus on completing and disseminating projects. My key achievements so far have been:

  • edge264, a state-of-the-art open-source H.264/AVC video decoder, used in production at Oku3D and in deployment at Netflix, and showcasing many innovative programming practices [FOSDEM'24, FOSDEM'25, FOSDEM'26].
  • Polyphony, the first UI toolkit demonstrating and explaining how to design a UI architecture with the Entity-Component-System model.

I work hard to explore unconventional paths, most of my spare time going into compulsively crafting code then sharing it with engineering communities.

On a personal note I am passionate about choir singing 🎶, dancing 🕺, and crafting 🛠.
